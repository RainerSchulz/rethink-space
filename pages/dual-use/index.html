Defense Options

What withstands radiation, temperature swings and impacts on the Moon also holds where conventional construction reaches its limits on Earth: hardened shelter, sealed structures, supply that works without a chain behind it.

We assess every development for its security-relevant use as well – and are transparent about where that assessment leads. Export control and end use are part of the design decision, not an afterthought.

Down Streaming

Space projects rarely justify themselves through their destination alone. If a development is usable terrestrially at the same time, that changes funding, schedule and risk.

Building without cement, on-site excavation as raw material, self-sufficient energy: the processes we qualify for the Moon reduce emissions and dependencies in ordinary construction too. Our IP covers both routes.
