Rationale

Not a by-product, but a design principle.

Space projects rarely justify themselves through their destination alone. If a development is usable terrestrially at the same time, that changes funding, schedule and risk.

That is why we assess every technology twice from the start: what does it achieve on the Moon – and where does it carry weight on Earth.
