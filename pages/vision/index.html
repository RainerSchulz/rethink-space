The Moon is not a destination. It is the first place where it will be decided how humans live and work permanently beyond Earth.

Position

Why we rethink space.

For decades space was a question of engineering. The next stage is a question of design: what do places look like in which people do not merely survive, but work, do research and recover?

RE-THINK SPACE combines architecture, materials research and mission planning into an approach that starts with people – and works back to the technology.

  • 01Building with local resources instead of costly transport logistics.
  • 02Structures that withstand radiation, temperature and micrometeorites.
  • 03Spaces that are psychologically sustainable – light, openness, retreat.
  • 04Every development with an impact on terrestrial applications.

The road ahead

From study to structure.

We work in stages: concept study, material qualification, demonstrator, mission integration. Each stage delivers results that also stand on their own.
