New Space Economy

“HUMANITY´S MOST AMBITIOUS BUILDING PROJECT.”

Private space ventures accelerate humanity´s path to the stars. More agile iteration cycles and independent private financing are fundamentally reshaping the space economy."

However, the main bottleneck that blocks humanity´s way to the moon (and the stars) is the immense payload to space. At a launch and landing cost of $1M per kilogram, surface habitat construction is economically viable only through high-degree In-Situ Resource Utilization (ISRU) - the practice of building with local lunar regolith. ISRU means using local lunar regolith for habitat construction, and it´s been a subject of researching for more than 40 years.

The best current concepts aim for 60% ISRU.

Thanks to the unique habitat design proposed by our LUNAR HABITATO project, we aim for up to 80% ISRU.

And it´s more than just a business case. Regarding the run for the first lunar nuclear plant, it´s a matter of national security as well as international stability for the USA. Hence, NASA strives to beat Russia and China to maintain its dominance in space.

The quest for energy is resolved with the installment of a compact fission reactor. The next crucial step will be the construction of a shelter for all the valuable tools that have to be protected from the abrasive regolith dust - rovers, drones, and robots.

The next bottleneck is computing power, preferably provided by a lunar data center. Lunar data centers are a matter of interest for the European Space Agency (ESA) as well. RETHINK SPACE (together with its development partner DC Smarter) has been selected for ESA ESTEC´s "Strategy Forum on Space-based Data Centers".

Only once these steps are taken, laboratories for lunar-specific researching and living habitats for astronauts can be installed.

RETHINK SPACE aims to think all these puzzle pieces together and molds them into one coherent, modular infrastructure that lays the foundation for the first permanent human presence on the moon.

LUNAR HABITATION

LUNAR HABITATO

After founding HABITATO as a 3D printing tiny house concept in 2024, we re-imagined our habitat design for the demands of lunar colonization in 2025.

That was the birth of our Moon habitation project LUNAR HABITATO.

By 2026, LUNAR HABITATO is no space tech rookie anymore.

With three WIPO-registered design IPs for our unique habitat design, two research projects and several LOIs from industrial and research partners, LUNAR HABITATO validates its bold claims constantly.

Rather than aiming for a lunar mission for us, LUNAR HABITATO orchestrates all the relevant space tech innovators and develops a unique deployment mission method for a modular lunar infrastructure. This roadmap will be offered as a licensing model that allows license partners to deploy an ultra-resilient, permanent moon habitat for a fraction of our competitor´s budgets.

Enabled by an unparalleled high ISRU degree and semi-autonomous swarm robotics, LUNAR HABITATO paths new ways to the moon and beyond.

Design & IP

“UNIQUE DESIGN SOLUTIONS FOR UNIQUE PROBLEMS.”

Everybody knows the Matryoshka dolls, the famous Russian nesting dolls.

This unique principle inspired our shell design.

With our three WIPO-registered design IPs we offer new ways to ISRU-based moon habitation. The “Matryoshka Shell Design” (MaSh) provides unparalleled structural integrity and radiation shielding through its double-layered, nested shell design.

But it doesn´t stop there.

By rethinking exterior geometry, we engineered a modular surface solution that maximizes surface area and structural deflection. Our novel knobbed exterior BUG SHIELDS ('Single Armor' & 'Double Armor') - provide enhanced protection against micrometeorite impacts.

“Bring the shovel to the sand. Not the other way around.”

That´s ISRU in a nutshell.

Every percent of gained ISRU degree equals a cost reduction of roughly one billion dollars.

Through our research programs, we demonstrate the feasibility of up to 80% ISRU level and offer a licensing model that reduces overall habitat deployment costs by a multitude.

“Intellectual property. Our most valuable asset.”

Deployment missions are dangerous.

Robots don´t need oxygen, food, or water.

They don´t care about gamma radiation. At least not as much as humans do.

So why put the life of astronauts in jeopardy, when robots can do the job?

To address these issues, our IP expands to a specific, scientifically validated methodology.

We plan to use brain-computer interfaces to control the semi-autonomous swarm-robotics. The mission controllers will be safe all the time.

Lunar digital twins provide early validation of this particular methodology.

These assets put us in a strong position to establish an innovative lunar habitat design.

Human safety above all!

WASP / ISA study “GLAMS”
In a joint study with the Italian Space Agency, WASP has demonstrated the possibility to gain the binding material for the 3D printing also from regolith, in form of geopolymers. Given that 10% of the habitat weight is binding material, this method alone literally saves a hundred billion Dollars!

From Q1/2027, RETHINK SPACE will commercialize researching as "Science as a Service" (ScaaS), offering customized feasibility studies and space researching for our industrial partners. Our RETHINK SPACE RESEARCH INSTITUTE will be more than just an early revenue stream; Scaas will further support our validation and contribute to our roadmap for an entire lunar infrastructure.
