Re-Think Deployment

From idea to landing site.

Strategic implementation for lasting presence on the Moon – logistics, sequence, operations.

Approach

Four phases to operation.

01 — Site

Selection by solar exposure, ground conditions, line of sight and proximity to resources. The site determines half of the mission economics.

02 — Preparation

Robotic units level, compact and start construction before humans arrive. At this stage errors are still correctable.

03 — Assembly

Erection of the primary structure, integration of pressure shell, power and life-support systems, leak testing.

04 — Operation

Handover to the crew, maintenance cycles, expansion with further modules. Every unit is planned to be expandable from the outset.

Collaboration

We work in partnership.

No single player brings launcher, lander, robotics and habitat together alone. We contribute the building side – aligned with our partners’ interfaces.

For agencies and industry that means defined masses, defined volumes and defined assembly times instead of declarations of intent.
