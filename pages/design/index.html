Design

Design that carries.

Innovative design for meaningful spaces beyond Earth – from dome geometry to interior.

Geometry

The dome distributes loads evenly and can be assembled from many small, identical elements – ideal for robotic on-site fabrication.

Material

Regolith as base material, complemented by transported binders. Less launch mass, more shielding – the environment becomes the building material.

Learn more

Interior

Zoning instead of confinement: work, community and retreat are spatially separated, daylight simulation stabilises the circadian rhythm.

Learn more

Principle

Form follows mission.

Every gram that does not have to be launched is payload gained. That is why the structure is built as far as possible from what is available on site.

At the same time a habitat has to do more than protect. It shapes how a crew works over months – here design is a safety factor.
